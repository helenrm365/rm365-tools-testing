Clocking Guide

1

Check the reader status

The badge in the scanner bar shows whether the NFC reader is connected. "Ready" means you are good to go.

2

Start or stop scanning

Scanning starts automatically when the page opens. Use the Start / Stop buttons to pause the terminal, e.g. outside working hours.

3

Tap a card to clock

Employees hold their NFC card on the reader. The system works out whether it is a clock in or clock out automatically.

4

Confirm the result

Each scan shows a result card under the scanner bar for a few seconds, and every record is listed in Recent Scans. Unregistered cards are flagged.

5

Review the day

"Last" and "Scans" in the scanner bar track today’s activity. For full history and exports, head to the Timesheets page.
