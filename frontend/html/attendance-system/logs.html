Timesheets Guide

1

Pick a date range

Records load for the last 7 days automatically. Change the From and To dates to look further back.

2

Narrow it down

Filter by location, employee name or action type (clock in / clock out) — results update automatically as you go.

3

Review the results

The summary cards at the top update with each search. Click any column header in the results table to sort.

4

Export or print

Once results are loaded, export them as CSV, PDF or Excel — or print directly. Excel exports can be split by week or month.
