Analytics Guide

1

Set your filters

Use the Report Filters panel to pick a date range (presets or custom dates), a location, or search a specific employee. Press Apply to refresh the page.

2

Know each section's scope

Real-Time Status always shows today, live. Punctuality & Compliance follows your filters. Attendance Analysis cards show today — its list view uses your date range.

3

Drill down into names

Click any stat card to jump to a list of the people behind that number. You can also switch any section between cards and list with the toggle in its header.

4

Check an employee's logs

In Attendance Analysis, click an employee's card to open their full clock-in/out history for the period.

5

Export your data

Every section has an Export dropdown in its header for PDF or CSV downloads. Employee cards have their own export menus too. For full timesheet exports, use the Timesheets tab.
